Precise-nearby Service discovery pagination contract

1. Customer precise coordinates are accepted only in the POST request body for /api/marketplace/services/nearby. They are not placed in the Explore URL, persisted as a Customer location record, or returned in the response.
2. The public nearby search RPC is SECURITY INVOKER and runs under the anonymous marketplace RLS boundary. Professional and Business Services remain in one unified result set.
3. Private geo tables remain inaccessible as public marketplace read surfaces. A non-exposed marketplace_internal helper may read private geo data only to derive Service distance inputs; raw Provider or Service coordinates are never returned by the public RPC or API route.
4. at_provider matching uses an active service_site point. at_customer matching prefers a matching-enabled, non-expired Provider live point and falls back to an active mobile_base only when no valid Provider live point exists.
5. at_customer max_travel_distance_meters remains authoritative. Services without an eligible geo match are not hidden solely because of distance; they remain eligible for ordinary marketplace discovery with distance priority 0, preserving the no-fixed-radius behavior.
6. Public responses expose only coarse distance_band, bounded distance_priority, and nearby_match_mode. Exact distance meters and raw coordinates are not exposed.
7. Nearby relevance preserves live availability, taxonomy aliases/groups, Provider name, rating/review, and the existing near-me distance boost. nearest sort prioritizes coarse distance, then live availability, relevance, rating, and review count.
8. Nearby discovery is bounded to 24 Services by default and uses a server cursor for additional pages. Query, category, named location, price, rating, Provider type, available-now, and supported sort filters are applied server-side.
9. If the nearby RPC is unavailable, the endpoint falls back to the bounded normal Service discovery RPC and returns geo_status=unavailable instead of broadening access or failing the marketplace solely because geo enrichment is unavailable.
10. No Service, Provider, review, availability, geo configuration, booking, order, finance, payment, payout, recurrence, recovery, or publication state is mutated by nearby discovery.
11. Provider identity finality remains unchanged: one account may have one final Provider identity, Professional OR Business, while customer discovery remains unified across both.
12. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remain HOLD. Recurrence/recovery remain FROZEN.
