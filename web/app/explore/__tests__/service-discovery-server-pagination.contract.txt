Service discovery server-pagination and Explore cutover contract

1. Normal and named-location customer Service discovery uses the bounded public /api/marketplace/services/search endpoint and loads at most 24 Services per page by default, with a server cursor for Load more.
2. Public eligibility remains governed by existing anonymous Services, Provider, Reviews, live-availability, Shop-state, and taxonomy RLS paths. The search RPC is SECURITY INVOKER and callable by anon, not directly by authenticated users.
3. Professional and Business Services remain in one unified discovery result set. Provider identity finality is unchanged.
4. Query, category, named location, price, rating, Provider type, available-now, and relevance/rating/price sort semantics are applied server-side for non-geo discovery. Intent-only terms such as near me and available now do not distort semantic relevance text.
5. Taxonomy aliases and category groups come from the existing public marketplace taxonomy projection, and category facets are not limited to the currently loaded page.
6. Changing non-geo query/filter/sort state restarts discovery from the first server page. Load more appends only results for the still-current search state and avoids duplicate Service ids.
7. Current-location precise nearby matching remains on the existing POST /api/marketplace/services geo path. The browser continues to apply the established local filters, distance bands, and nearest/relevance ranking to that full geo response; the server-pagination cutover does not redefine geo semantics.
8. Clearing current location returns Explore to the paginated server-search path. Precise coordinates are not added to the page URL or persisted by this discovery flow.
9. No Service, Provider, review, availability, booking, order, finance, payment, payout, recurrence, or publication state is mutated by discovery search.
10. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remain HOLD and recurrence/recovery remain FROZEN.
