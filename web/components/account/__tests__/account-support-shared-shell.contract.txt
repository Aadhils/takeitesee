Account support shared shell contract

1. Authenticated /account/support renders inside LocalizedAccountShell with /account/support active.
2. The unauthenticated support fallback remains outside the authenticated shell and preserves sign-in returnTo=/account/support.
3. The Grievance Officer email fallback remains mailto:uandv.com@gmail.com.
4. Support request loading, submission, history, request types and /api/account/support-requests behavior remain unchanged.
5. The Account privacy link remains available from the support form.
6. No database, RLS, auth model, booking, provider, finance or recurrence/recovery behavior is changed.
7. Signed-in visual acceptance requires authenticated browser evidence and must not be inferred from source or CI alone.
