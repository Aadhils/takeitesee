Live customer reviews center contract

1. /reviews must use authenticated live booking and review data rather than discovery fixtures or presentation-only review cards.
2. An unauthenticated visitor must receive a sign-in/create-account state and must not see private booking or review data.
3. GET /api/reviews without bookingId must return only reviews authored by the authenticated customer, ordered newest first.
4. Existing GET /api/reviews?bookingId=... behavior must remain compatible for live booking detail review reads.
5. The reviews center must show completed bookings from the configured live customer booking repository and associate submitted reviews by booking_id.
6. A completed booking without a submitted review must not be labelled review-eligible by the center. It must link to the live booking detail to check server-authoritative review-window eligibility and deadline.
7. Submitted rating/comment and any provider response may be displayed, but customer-authored review text must not be altered.
8. Review creation remains exclusively server-authoritative through POST /api/reviews, completed-booking ownership checks and the closeout review-window gate.
9. This slice is read-side UI/API expansion only: no review, booking, Provider, service, job, messaging, notification or support state is mutated by loading /reviews.
10. No database migration or RLS/auth model change is introduced.
11. One account = Customer + one Provider identity remains authoritative.
12. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remains HOLD.
13. Recurrence/recovery remains FROZEN.
14. Supabase Pro leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
15. Signed-in visual/interaction acceptance must not be claimed without authenticated evidence; code, CI, unauthenticated privacy state and runtime verification may be completed independently.
