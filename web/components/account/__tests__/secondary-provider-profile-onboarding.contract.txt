Secondary provider profile onboarding contract — SUPERSEDED

The dual-provider same-login behavior introduced by the earlier secondary-profile work is no longer authoritative.

Authoritative product rule from migration 20260905230426 and the single-provider identity contract:
- one TakeItEsee account always retains its Customer identity;
- the account may optionally own ONE Provider identity: Professional OR Business, never both;
- after one Provider identity is approved, the opposite Provider type requires a separate TakeItEsee account;
- a pending Provider application reserves the choice until it is withdrawn, rejected, or approved;
- database ownership triggers, onboarding submission, Admin approval, account UI, and onboarding UI must all enforce the same invariant.
