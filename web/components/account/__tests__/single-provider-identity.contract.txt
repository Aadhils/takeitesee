Single Provider Identity per Account contract

1. Every TakeItEsee account retains its Customer identity and may optionally own exactly one Provider identity: Professional OR Business, never both.
2. A customer-only account with no pending Provider application may choose either Professional or Business.
3. A pending Provider application reserves that selected type; the opposite type is not offered while review is pending. The applicant may withdraw before approval and choose again.
4. Once a Professional identity exists, the same account cannot submit, approve, insert, or switch into a Business identity. A separate TakeItEsee account is required for Business.
5. Once a Business identity exists, the same account cannot submit, approve, insert, or switch into a Professional identity. A separate TakeItEsee account is required for Professional.
6. Database authority is layered: cross-table ownership triggers block direct dual ownership, Business ownership is unique per user, submit_provider_application blocks any second Provider application after ownership, and review_provider_application blocks stale conflicting approvals.
7. Concurrency-sensitive ownership/submission/approval checks serialize on the account user id with a transaction advisory lock.
8. /account presents "Start earning on TakeItEsee" only before a Provider identity is selected; it explains the exclusive Professional-or-Business choice before onboarding.
9. /provider/onboarding requires an explicit acknowledgement that only one Provider identity can be registered on the account before submission.
10. After approval, /account and /provider/onboarding show the selected Provider identity and explain that the opposite type requires a separate account; they do not advertise an add-another-provider action.
11. Customer-only /account must not render a second legacy "Provider workspace / Start providing services" entry below the exclusive Professional-or-Business chooser; the chooser is the single authoritative Provider entry point.
12. On mobile, the fixed primary bottom navigation must reserve enough bottom clearance that footer/legal content can be scrolled fully above the navigation and remains readable/tappable.
13. Admin review shows existing Provider identity context. Any pending application against an account that already owns either Provider type is a Provider identity conflict; Approve is disabled while Reject remains available. Database guards remain authoritative.
14. Existing Customer functionality remains active alongside the selected Provider identity. Admin/Super Admin permissions remain separate permitted platform workspaces and do not count as a second Provider identity.
15. Provider verification remains a separate review after onboarding approval. Verification/trust/service launch semantics are otherwise unchanged.
16. Production had zero dual-provider owning accounts and zero pending Provider applications before migration 20260905230426, so no destructive provider-identity conversion was required.
17. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery activation remains HOLD. Recurrence/recovery remains FROZEN. Supabase Pro leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
