Live booking review route parity contract

1. /bookings/{bookingId}/review must not depend on discovery/demo fixture bookings.
2. The route must redirect to /bookings/{bookingId}, where the live CustomerBookingDetail owns review eligibility, deadline, existing review state, rating/comment submission, and closeout context.
3. /reviews/{bookingId} and /bookings/{bookingId}/review must converge on the same live booking detail review experience.
4. Real production booking UUIDs must no longer 404 merely because they are absent from presentation fixtures.
5. Review submission remains server-authoritative through /api/reviews and the live booking closeout review window.
6. No review is created or mutated by the redirect itself.
7. Booking ownership, completion state, review-window rules, provider responses, support/closeout behavior, and review moderation remain unchanged.
8. No database migration, RLS, authentication, Provider identity, service, job, messaging, notification, or finance state changes are introduced.
9. One account = Customer + one Provider identity remains authoritative.
10. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remains HOLD.
11. Recurrence/recovery remains FROZEN.
12. Supabase Pro leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
