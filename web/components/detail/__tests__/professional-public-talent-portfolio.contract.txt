Professional public talent portfolio contract

1. A public professional profile remains one verified `professional_profiles` identity.
2. Only active `professional_roles` visible through the existing public RLS boundary may appear on `/professionals/[providerId]`.
3. Inactive/private owner roles must not be exposed by the public profile query.
4. Public role presentation may show title, summary, experience years and declared opportunity signals only.
5. Opportunity signals (service bookings, freelance, part-time, full-time, contract) are profile presentation metadata; they do not grant booking, application or employment eligibility.
6. Existing active service listings remain the only service-booking CTAs in this slice.
7. Business public profiles keep their existing behavior and receive no professional-role section.
8. SEO metadata may use public talent titles and structured-data `knowsAbout`, but must not expose private role data.
9. Provider legal disclosure and grievance presentation remain unchanged.
10. No subscription, ranking, job application, resume, portfolio media, recurrence or finance behavior is introduced here.
11. Cash on Service remains unchanged. Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/disputes/collections remain HOLD.
12. Supabase Pro / leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
