Employer Applicant Finder contract

1. Business provider jobs workspace exposes a dedicated Applicant Finder entry point.
2. Applicant Finder is Business-only and refuses Professional-mode workspace data.
3. Finder uses the existing authenticated /api/provider/job-marketplace payload; no new database or API write surface is introduced.
4. Keyword search matches available applicant headline, service area, job title, cover note and application stage context.
5. Employer can filter applicants by job posting, current application stage and Professional verification state.
6. Employer can sort matching applicants by newest, oldest, Professional headline A-Z or job title A-Z.
7. Filtered result count and clear-filter controls distinguish no applicants from no matching applicants.
8. Candidate cards preserve profile, conversation and hiring-workspace navigation without mutating application status.
9. Existing application/interview/offer/message/job lifecycle semantics remain unchanged.
10. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remains HOLD.
11. Recurrence/recovery remains FROZEN.
12. Supabase Pro leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
