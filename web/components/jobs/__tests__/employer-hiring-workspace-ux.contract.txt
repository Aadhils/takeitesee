Employer Hiring Workspace UX contract

1. Business accounts use the polished Hiring Workspace; Professional job workspace behavior remains unchanged.
2. Backend job, application, interview, offer, messaging, moderation, resume-snapshot and hiring consent APIs are reused without schema/state-machine changes.
3. Hiring overview exposes Open jobs, Applicants, Upcoming interviews and Pending offers as actionable summary cards.
4. Business workflow is organized into Jobs, Applicants, Interviews and Offers tabs.
5. Create-job form is hidden by default and opened by a clear + Create job action.
6. Job form groups Job basics, Requirements and Compensation instead of exposing one undifferentiated long form.
7. Initial-status dropdown is removed from UI; explicit Save draft and Publish job actions send the existing draft/open statuses.
8. Publish is disabled for an unverified Business; Save draft remains available subject to existing backend rules.
9. Required-skill text remains API-compatible while rendering entered values as preview chips.
10. Job cards surface status, employment/workplace type, location, compensation, openings, deadline and applicant count.
11. Applicant tab preserves existing stage transitions and never reintroduces direct employer Hired action.
12. Interview tab preserves existing schedule/reschedule/cancel APIs and audit history.
13. Offers tab reuses JobOfferWorkspace and two-party applicant acceptance from #248.
14. Employer Applicant Resume Review remains available inside Applicants.
15. Layout is responsive: four KPI cards on desktop, two-column summary on small screens, single-column forms on mobile.
16. Finance/Cashfree, recurrence/recovery and Supabase Pro leaked-password holds are untouched.
