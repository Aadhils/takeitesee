Employer + Job Opportunity Marketplace Foundation contract

1. Existing verified Business identity is the employer identity; no duplicate employer account is created.
2. Existing verified Professional identity is the applicant identity; no duplicate applicant account is created.
3. Public job board exposes only open, in-deadline jobs from verified businesses.
4. Business owners can create draft jobs; only verified businesses can publish open jobs.
5. Professionals must be verified before applying and cannot apply to their own business job.
6. One Professional may submit at most one application per job posting.
7. Applicant may only withdraw an active application; they cannot self-promote status.
8. Employer status transitions are controlled: submitted -> shortlisted/interview/rejected; shortlisted -> interview/rejected; interview -> hired/rejected.
9. Job/application ownership is enforced by RLS; anon receives no application access.
10. Salary data is descriptive employment compensation only and does not activate payments, payouts, settlement or Cashfree.
11. Subscription/search-priority boosts are outside this slice.
12. Resume/Career and Portfolio remain independent existing profile modules; this slice does not rewrite them.
13. Recurring requirement/recovery lifecycle remains frozen and unchanged.
