Job application communication + interview scheduling contract

1. Reuse the existing marketplace conversation/message/read-marker/block transport. Do not introduce a second chat system.
2. A job application conversation is created only after the employer moves an application to shortlisted or interview.
3. The job conversation participants are exactly the applicant professional user and the owning business employer user.
4. Submitted applications have no private conversation. Hired, rejected and withdrawn applications close the existing conversation as read-only history.
5. Job messaging is writable only while the application is shortlisted or interview and the conversation is open/unblocked.
6. Existing requirement conversations retain their awarded-provider messaging rules unchanged.
7. A verified business employer may schedule an interview only while the application status is interview.
8. The employer may reschedule or cancel. The applicant may accept or decline a scheduled interview but cannot change schedule details.
9. At most one non-cancelled interview row may exist per application. Rescheduling updates the same row.
10. Interview lifecycle changes create participant-readable audit snapshots and notifications.
11. Interview rows/events are participant-only under RLS. Anonymous and unrelated authenticated users receive no interview access.
12. Job conversations reuse block/unblock safety. Requirement-scoped moderation/reporting remains unchanged; job-specific moderation is outside this slice.
13. No subscription, search-ranking, recruiter billing, finance/Cashfree, recurring requirement/recovery, or Supabase Pro behavior changes are part of this slice.
