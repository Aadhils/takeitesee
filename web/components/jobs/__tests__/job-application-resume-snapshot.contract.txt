Job Application Resume Snapshot + Employer Review contract

1. Every newly submitted job application captures exactly one immutable career-only resume snapshot through the database trigger.
2. The snapshot belongs to the job application and is readable only by the applicant and the Business owner of that job posting.
3. Anonymous users and unrelated authenticated users cannot read an application snapshot.
4. Browser-authenticated users cannot insert, update, or delete snapshot rows directly; snapshot capture is database-controlled.
5. Later edits to Professional career/resume data do not mutate the historical application snapshot.
6. The Professional public_resume_enabled setting is neither required nor modified by applying; job-application sharing is a separate explicit disclosure context.
7. Snapshot data may include career headline/summary, availability, selected talent, skills, experience, education, and certifications.
8. Snapshot data must not include phone/email contact fields, legal/KYC fields, grievance details, payment/finance data, payout data, or settlement data.
9. Employer applicant review renders the frozen application snapshot alongside job/application status and cover note.
10. Existing job application status, private messaging, interview scheduling, moderation, and public Professional profile behavior remain unchanged.
11. Finance/Cashfree remains HOLD, recurring requirement/recovery remains FROZEN, and Supabase Pro leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
