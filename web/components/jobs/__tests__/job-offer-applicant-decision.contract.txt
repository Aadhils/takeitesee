Job offer + applicant decision contract

1. A verified Business employer may issue an employment offer only for its own application while that application is in interview status.
2. An issued offer is immutable. Changing terms requires the employer to withdraw the pending offer and issue a revised version.
3. At most one pending offer may exist per application. Revised offers receive the next offer_number automatically.
4. Only the Professional applicant and owning Business employer may read an offer.
5. Only the applicant may accept or decline a pending offer. Only the employer may withdraw a pending offer.
6. Employer-side direct interview -> hired transitions are forbidden.
7. Applicant acceptance of a pending offer atomically changes the application from interview -> hired.
8. Declining or employer-withdrawing an offer leaves the application in interview so revised terms can be issued.
9. Rejecting or withdrawing the application automatically closes any pending offer.
10. Existing job conversation lifecycle remains authoritative: accepted offer -> hired closes the application conversation as hired.
11. Offer lifecycle notifications are participant-scoped for issued, accepted, declined, and employer-withdrawn events.
12. Offer compensation is informational employment-term data only. It does not activate Cashfree, payroll, payment collection, refunds, payouts, settlement, reconciliation, disputes, or finance recovery.
13. Finance/Cashfree HOLD, recurring requirement/recovery FROZEN scope, and Supabase Pro leaked-password protection HOLD remain unchanged.
14. Pre-persistence rollback verification covers direct-hire blocking, participant RLS, outsider blocking, immutable terms, one pending offer, accept -> hired, decline, revised offer numbering, employer withdrawal, application withdrawal cleanup, existing chat closure, and notifications.
15. The corrected rollback verification matrix is 15/15 PASS; the initial single false result was an assertion-pattern error and left zero rollback residue.
