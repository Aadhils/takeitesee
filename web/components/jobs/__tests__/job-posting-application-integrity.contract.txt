Job posting application integrity contract

1. A Business may continue to change ordinary job-posting terms only while the posting has no applications.
2. The first persisted job application locks the posting terms that applicants relied on: title, description, employment type, workplace type, location, required skills, minimum experience, openings, compensation fields, and application deadline.
3. After the first application, the owning Business cannot mutate any locked posting term through the Data API or future app-server edit paths.
4. Job lifecycle status remains independently mutable through the existing controlled employer workflow; closing, reopening, or marking filled must not rewrite the locked terms.
5. Platform moderation remains independent: Admin pause/restore may still change moderation state and force/retain draft status without rewriting job terms.
6. A job posting with one or more applications cannot be deleted, because deletion must not cascade-delete application, resume-snapshot, conversation, interview, offer, moderation, or audit evidence.
7. A job posting with zero applications may still be deleted under the existing owning-Business RLS policy.
8. The owning Business cannot reassign business ownership or mutate server-controlled moderation fields; existing #258 column-privilege boundaries remain authoritative.
9. The integrity guard must be SECURITY INVOKER with an empty search_path and direct EXECUTE revoked from PUBLIC, anon, and authenticated.
10. Existing application/interview/offer state machines are unchanged; no new employment transition is introduced.
11. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remain HOLD; recurring requirement/recovery remains FROZEN; Supabase Pro leaked-password work remains HOLD.
