Professional saved jobs contract

1. Only an authenticated user who owns a Professional identity may read, save, or remove Professional saved jobs.
2. Saving a job does not require Professional verification; the existing verified-Professional gate for submitting a job application remains unchanged.
3. A newly saved job must currently be an open, moderation-clear, non-expired opportunity from a verified Business that is not owned by the saving user.
4. Saving the same job more than once is idempotent and cannot create duplicate saved rows.
5. If a saved job later closes, is paused, or expires, the private saved record may remain and the Professional workspace must present it as unavailable rather than as an actionable public job.
6. Permanently deleting a zero-application job cascades its saved-job relation so no orphan bookmark remains.
7. Row-level security permits only the owning Professional to read or delete their saved-job rows; anon has no table privileges.
8. Public /jobs exposes Save job / Saved controls only when the current signed-in account has a Professional identity; guests and non-Professional accounts retain the existing public job experience.
9. The Professional Jobs workspace includes a Saved jobs tab that lists available and unavailable saved opportunities and supports removing them.
10. Saved-job actions do not change job publication, moderation, application, interview, offer, messaging, finance, recurrence, or recovery state.
