Safe employer job deletion contract

1. Only the authenticated owning Business may delete one of its own job postings through the supported server route.
2. A job with zero applications may be permanently deleted.
3. Once the first application exists, the supported API must reject deletion with a conflict response.
4. The #259 database trigger remains the race-safe final invariant if an application arrives between the API pre-check and DELETE.
5. The Business jobs workspace must expose deletion only for zero-application jobs; jobs with applicants must never show a destructive delete action.
6. Deletion must require an explicit second confirmation step and explain that the action is permanent.
7. Deleting an open zero-application job removes it from public discovery by deleting the owned posting; no applicant/hiring evidence exists to cascade.
8. Job status, edit, applicant, interview, offer and moderation lifecycles remain unchanged.
9. Professional jobs workspace must not expose Business job deletion controls.
10. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remains untouched; recurrence/recovery remains FROZEN.
