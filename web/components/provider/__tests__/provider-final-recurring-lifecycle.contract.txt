Provider final recurring lifecycle contract

- Provider requirement context exposes provider-safe requirement_status and job_state after ownership is authenticated.
- A fulfilled recurring requirement must be identifiable as fulfilled in provider requirement context.
- A fulfilled occurrence must expose job_state=fulfilled.
- Normal fulfilled occurrences must not carry recovery metadata.
- Recovered fulfilled occurrences must preserve recovery attempt and prior-booking context.
- Customer occurrence plan and provider occurrence context must agree on final fulfilled occurrence states.
- Customer recovery history and provider recovered-occurrence context must agree on the completed recovery audit.
- Unrelated providers must remain blocked from booking requirement context.
- Provider final-state presentation is read-only and must not change recurrence sequencing or recovery eligibility.
- Cash on Service behavior is unchanged.
- Cashfree, online gateway, refunds, payouts, settlement and reconciliation remain HOLD.
- Supabase Pro / leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
