Provider trust recovery -> marketplace re-entry contract

1. Generic trust restore is valid only for a suspended Provider. A re-verification requirement can return to normal only when a fresh provider verification request is approved.
2. Re-verification and suspension pause active Services. For Business Providers they also pause active Business Products.
3. A Business Product cannot transition to catalog status Active while Provider trust is not normal, even through a direct authenticated product update.
4. Trust restoration never auto-reactivates paused Services or Products. The Provider must review and explicitly reactivate each offering after recovery.
5. Existing bookings, history, support, closeout, Provider ownership, verification evidence, product launch approval history and identity handles are preserved.
6. After trust returns to normal, base public profile/@handle/search eligibility continues to depend on the existing verification, disclosure, profile and public-readiness gates.
7. Existing approved Product revision remains revision-bound; a trust pause does not manufacture a new approval or bypass product launch review.
8. Professional and Business remain separate final Provider identities while unified marketplace discovery remains unchanged.
9. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remain HOLD.
10. Recurrence/recovery remain frozen.
