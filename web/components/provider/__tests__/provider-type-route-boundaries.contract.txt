Provider type-specific route boundary contract

Authoritative identity rule
- One account keeps Customer plus ONE Provider identity: Professional OR Business, never both.
- Shared Provider routes may serve both Provider types when their product behavior is intentionally common or type-aware.
- Type-specific routes must not render the opposite Provider module merely because the URL was manually addressed.

Read-side guard
- Type-specific Provider child pages use getProviderSessionOrNull().
- The helper is non-throwing so the parent /provider layout remains the authoritative guest/non-provider redirect boundary.
- The helper re-derives Provider roles from the authenticated server session and preserves the legacy active-workspace preference defensively if both Provider roles are ever present.
- No cookie or page guard grants Provider authorization; existing API/RPC/RLS/ownership checks remain authoritative.

Professional-only routes
- /provider/portfolio requires the active Professional role; Business redirects to /provider.
- /provider/resume requires the active Professional role; Business redirects to /provider.
- /provider/resume/export requires the active Professional role; Business redirects to /provider.

Business-only routes
- /provider/jobs/applicants requires the active Business owner role; Professional redirects to /provider/jobs, which renders the Professional jobs workspace.

Shared routes intentionally remain shared/type-aware
- /provider
- /provider/public-readiness (Professional public-profile visibility and Business public-storefront visibility are evaluated with their current type-specific public route contracts)
- /provider/setup
- /provider/leads
- /provider/messages
- /provider/bookings
- /provider/schedule
- /provider/services
- /provider/verification
- /provider/earnings
- /provider/reviews
- /provider/profile
- /provider/jobs (Business employer workflow vs Professional jobs workflow is selected by the existing live job-marketplace mode)

Public readiness semantics
- Professional public-profile visibility requires profile basics, verification, and marketplace disclosure, matching the existing Professional public loader.
- Business public-storefront visibility requires verification and marketplace disclosure, matching the existing Business public loader.
- Service/role/career content is shown as discovery content rather than being mislabeled as a base public-profile visibility requirement.
- Business service activation still requires Business profile basics through the existing service-launch contract.
- Provider navigation must not offer a public-profile/storefront link unless the corresponding public-profile readiness state is satisfied and the existing trust navigation guard is normal.

Scope boundaries
- No database migration, RLS, Provider identity, onboarding, verification, trust, service, booking, job application/interview/offer, moderation, or notification state-machine change.
- Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remains HOLD.
- Recurrence/recovery remains FROZEN.
- Supabase leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
