Recurring final fulfillment / closure contract

1. A recurring requirement remains awarded while fewer than recurrence_count occurrences are fulfilled.
2. A recovered occurrence keeps its original sequence number and can still contribute one fulfilled occurrence after the existing completion + customer confirmation + payment-settled gates pass.
3. Normal later occurrences advance sequentially and do not inherit recovery metadata from an earlier recovered occurrence.
4. Completing the final required occurrence changes the requirement from awarded to fulfilled.
5. Final requirement fulfillment sets closed_at exactly through the existing fulfillment synchronization path.
6. Once the requirement is fulfilled, customers cannot create a sequence beyond recurrence_count and no extra marketplace_requirement_jobs row may appear.
7. Completed requirement_occurrence_recoveries audit history remains intact through later occurrence creation and final requirement closure.
8. Provider context for the final normal occurrence reports the final occurrence number and recovery=null.
9. Final-fulfillment verification must be rollback-only and leave no synthetic requirement, proposal, booking, job, closeout or recovery rows behind.
10. Cash on Service behavior is unchanged.
11. Cashfree, online gateway, refunds, payouts, settlement and reconciliation remain HOLD and are not activated by this contract.
12. Supabase Pro / leaked-password protection work remains HOLD.
