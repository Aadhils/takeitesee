Recurring final-state UI contract

1. A fulfilled recurring requirement must show the customer service-job panel top status as Fulfilled, never Not scheduled merely because no live job remains.
2. The existing fulfilled occurrence plan remains visible and read-only after final closure.
3. Final-state occurrence copy must explain that all planned occurrences are complete rather than suggesting another occurrence can be booked.
4. The job creation form must remain unavailable once requirement status is fulfilled.
5. Recovery history remains visible after final fulfillment.
6. A fulfilled recurring requirement must present recovery as read-only audit history and must not present a retry action.
7. Existing completed recovery links to prior cancelled and replacement bookings remain available.
8. No customer/provider booking lifecycle, occurrence sequencing, recovery eligibility, payment, Cash on Service, Cashfree, refund, payout, settlement or reconciliation behavior is changed by this UI-only slice.
9. No Supabase migration is required for this final-state presentation hardening.
