Recurring occurrence recovery foundation contract

- Recovery applies only to awarded recurring requirements owned by the authenticated customer.
- Only the latest cancelled/declined occurrence can be retried.
- Retry keeps the same occurrence sequence number; it does not consume or skip to the next occurrence.
- Any active/service_completed occurrence blocks recovery.
- The failed booking must already be cancelled.
- Only an unpaid cancelled booking can be auto-retried; any pending, paid, failed, refunded or future non-unpaid payment state requires support review.
- The prior booking is preserved for audit and its idempotency key is archived before creating the replacement booking.
- A recovery audit record links prior and replacement booking/job IDs.
- Replacement job IDs are preserved as audit UUIDs without a restrictive FK so a later cancelled replacement can be recovered again at the same occurrence sequence.
- The existing customer_create_requirement_job RPC remains authoritative for schedule, provider availability, blackout, overlap, duration and quote allocation checks.
- Cash on Service behavior is unchanged.
- Cashfree, online gateway, refund, payout, settlement and reconciliation behavior is unchanged and remains on hold.
