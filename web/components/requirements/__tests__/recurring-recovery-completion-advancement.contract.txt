Recurring recovery completion -> next occurrence advancement contract

1. Completing a recovered recurring occurrence never advances its occurrence number; the recovered job remains the same sequence until fulfilled.
2. A completed recovered occurrence becomes fulfilled only after the existing customer completion confirmation and payment-settled closeout gates pass.
3. Customer completion confirmation must trigger requirement-job fulfillment without requiring an unrelated later booking update.
4. A recurring requirement remains awarded while fewer than recurrence_count occurrences are fulfilled.
5. After recovered sequence #1 is fulfilled, the next customer-created job advances exactly to sequence #2.
6. Sequence #2 uses the existing planned-occurrence helper and selected-weekday/recurrence-interval scheduling gates.
7. Sequence #3 must not be created implicitly when sequence #2 is created.
8. Completed recovery audit rows for sequence #1 remain unchanged after fulfillment and next-occurrence creation.
9. Provider context for the recovered sequence keeps its recovery attempt metadata.
10. Provider context for a normal next occurrence has the new occurrence number and recovery=null.
11. sync_requirement_job_booking_state() and sync_requirement_job_from_closeout() keep SECURITY DEFINER behavior but use an empty search_path.
12. The rollback-only E2E may synthesize a settled completion state but must isolate finance/payment notification/reconciliation triggers and leave zero fixture residue.
13. Cash on Service behavior is unchanged.
14. Cashfree, online gateway, refund, payout, settlement, reconciliation and provider finance work remain HOLD.
15. Supabase Pro / leaked-password protection work remains HOLD.
