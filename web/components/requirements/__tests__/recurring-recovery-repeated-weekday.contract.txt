Recurring recovery repeated-attempt + weekday regression contract

- Repeated recovery may retry the same failed recurring occurrence more than once only through the audited recovery flow.
- Every successful retry preserves the failed occurrence sequence number; recovery must never advance to a later occurrence.
- Recovery audit history must form a chain: original cancelled booking -> first replacement -> second replacement.
- Provider recovered-occurrence context must report attempt_number=2 for the second completed recovery and point to the immediately prior failed replacement booking.
- Weekly recurring requirements with recurrence_weekdays must create/recover jobs only on one of those selected weekdays.
- When recurrence_interval > 1, selected-weekday jobs must also stay inside an active recurrence week measured from needed_by.
- Rejected weekday/interval recovery attempts must leave no recovery-audit residue and must preserve the current cancelled job.
- The centralized customer_create_requirement_job schedule gate remains authoritative for normal occurrence creation and recovery replacements.
- Provider availability, blackout, overlap, duration and quote checks remain authoritative after the recurrence schedule gate.
- Automatic recovery remains limited to cancelled + unpaid occurrences; any payment activity still requires support review.
- Cash on Service behavior is unchanged.
- Cashfree / online gateway / refund / payout / settlement / reconciliation remain HOLD.
- Supabase Pro / leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
