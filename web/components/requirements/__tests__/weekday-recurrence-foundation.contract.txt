Universal Services selected-weekday recurrence foundation

- Weekly recurring requirements may optionally carry selected weekdays as 0=Sunday through 6=Saturday.
- The first needed-by date must match one selected weekday.
- Existing weekly requirements with no selected-weekday array keep legacy same-weekday weekly behavior.
- Daily and monthly recurring requirements do not accept selected weekdays.
- Selected weekday arrays are normalized and idempotency-safe.
- Customer occurrence plans derive ordered dates across selected weekdays and recurrence intervals.
- Sequential requirement-job creation uses the same planned-date helper and keeps existing availability, blackout, conflict, payment, closeout and Cash-on-Service gates.
- No Cashfree or online finance activation is introduced.
