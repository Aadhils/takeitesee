Public job posting safety moderation contract

1. Public verified Business job postings are reportable by signed-in marketplace users.
2. A Business owner cannot report their own job posting.
3. Only currently public-eligible jobs are reportable: verified Business, open status, unexpired deadline, moderation state clear.
4. Duplicate open/reviewing reports for the same reporter and job posting are rejected.
5. Job posting reports use a dedicated job_posting moderation context and do not alter job application/message moderation semantics.
6. Platform-scope Admin/Super Admin can review job posting reports; delegated category/location Admin access is not expanded.
7. Report status and job visibility are separate controls.
8. Admin Pause sets the job moderation state to paused and returns the job to draft immediately, removing it from public discovery.
9. While moderation state is paused, the Business owner cannot republish the job, including through direct Data API updates.
10. Admin Restore clears the moderation lock but does not reopen the job; the Business must explicitly publish again.
11. Pause/Restore each require a note, write a content_action audit event, and notify the Business owner.
12. Admin Actioned/Dismissed closes only the report audit lifecycle and does not automatically ban/suspend the Business.
13. Existing applications, resume snapshots, conversations, interviews, offers and hiring state machines remain unchanged.
14. Finance/Cashfree/payment/refund/payout/settlement/reconciliation/recovery remain HOLD.
15. Recurring requirement/recovery remains FROZEN and Supabase Pro leaked-password protection remains HOLD.
